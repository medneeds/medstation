- Intern courtesies are granted only through an admin-checked server function that reuses the single courtesy row per user and sends the welcome email itself; this keeps grant and email in one place and stops the browser choosing recipients.
- Admin billing treats active paid one-time purchases (annual and Pix 30 days) as paying records through admin-list-subscribers/one-time.ts, and a signup trigger links purchases made before account creation by email; the annual plan is a one-time payment, not a Stripe subscription, so subscription-only counts miss it.
- One-time plans in supabase/functions/_shared/one-time-purchase.ts carry an `offered` flag and their own payment method list; checkout functions refuse plans not offered and fall back to card when an async method (boleto/Pix) is not activated, and access is granted only by the webhook once the payment is confirmed, so async methods never unlock access early.
- The Arsenal Med annual bonus is registered by push-plus-verify: stripe-webhook notifies the Arsenal Med site with the payment reference and that site confirms it through the public arsenal-bonus-verify function; failed notices are replayed for all current annual subscribers through the admin-only admin-resend-arsenal-bonus function, so this links two separate projects without sharing a secret.
- Admins set a password directly (and confirm the e-mail) only through the admin-checked admin-set-password function, with rules in supabase/functions/_shared/admin-password.ts and an audit_log entry; this covers e-mail delivery failures without exposing passwords in logs.
- Paid access resolves by the Stripe customer e-mail OR by Stripe customers already linked to the account's user_id (stripe_subscriptions), the webhook invites/links an account for every confirmed payment that has none, and admin-purchase-recovery audits active payments against accounts (rules in supabase/functions/_shared/payment-link.ts); payer and account e-mails differ in real life, so e-mail alone must never decide access.
- One-time annual purchases may be bought over an active monthly subscription: the webhook cancels the monthly at period end (owing ones immediately) and starts the 12 months after the last paid day, with the rule in supabase/functions/_shared/annual-switch.ts; boleto reminders are per-purchase (boleto-reminders, hourly cron, rule in _shared/boleto-reminder.ts) and campaign metrics come from the admin-only admin-black-dashboard function; this avoids double charges and keeps list messaging out of the app e-mail sender used for login codes.
- Post-purchase activation never asks for a password the buyer never created: complete-checkout uses checkoutAccountDecision in _shared/purchase-activation.ts (account invited by a payment, `payment_account_setup` + `payment_plan` in user metadata, never signed in/confirmed = `needsActivation`), /obrigado then shows "Enviamos seu acesso" with a 60 s rate-limited magic-link resend (signInWithOtp, shouldCreateUser false, redirect /onboarding?ativacao=compra) plus Google, and auth-email-hook swaps the generic invite (and that resend) for the "Pagamento confirmado: ative seu acesso" template only after checking the user's payment metadata; access itself still comes only from stripe-webhook.
- Black checkouts left unfinished get two automatic e-mails (black-recovery-reminders, hourly pg_cron job registered in drizzle/migrations/0010_black_recovery_emails.sql): templates black-recovery-1h (1–3 h after the last click/attempt) and black-recovery-24h (24–30 h), with "who gets what" in the pure _shared/black-recovery.ts (same abandoned rule as admin-black-dashboard; only during the offer; never to buyers of any paid annual/Black or active subscription, suppressed_emails, test addresses or the owner), each send claimed first in black_recovery_emails unique(email, step) and released only if the send throws, and every e-mail carries a /descadastrar link (email_unsubscribe_tokens → email-unsubscribe → suppressed_emails); this keeps the rule testable and makes double sends impossible even with overlapping runs.
- note-spellcheck returns the doctor's text unchanged when its import-free guard.ts sees any change to numbers, line count or size; this keeps doses and values safe from the AI.
- WhatsApp recovery (Bird) for doctors who used the free trial or once paid and do not pay now (never courtesy accounts): who enters, the 3-step cadence (day 0/+3/+7, Mon–Sat 9–20h Fortaleza, daily new-contact cap) and when it stops live in the pure _shared/whatsapp-recovery.ts; _shared/whatsapp-recovery-engine.ts re-checks real access with resolveUserAccess right before every send, claims each send in whatsapp_recovery_messages unique(contact_id, step) and releases it only on transient errors; whatsapp-recovery-run (hourly pg_cron, no-op while the whatsapp_recovery_auto flag is off), admin-whatsapp-recovery (panel actions, audit_log) and bird-whatsapp-webhook (Standard Webhooks signature, one row per event in bird_webhook_events; replies move the contact to manual conversation, SAIR/"não tenho interesse" goes to whatsapp_opt_outs) share it; setup and template copy in docs/whatsapp-recuperacao.md; this keeps paying users and opt-outs out of WhatsApp even with overlapping runs.
- The admin "Mapa de uso" (/admin/mapa) reads only counts from the staff-checked admin-usage-map function; each account's state comes from the profile state, then CRM state, then phone DDD, and cities only from the profile, with the rules in the pure _shared/usage-map.ts; this shows where MedStation is used without sending names, e-mails or phones to the browser.
